How to play

Goal

Tag opponents with your laser. First to 15 tags — or top score at 3:00 — wins.

Shields

Three hits tags you out. Break contact for 2 seconds and your shield fully recharges — firing keeps you in combat, so disengaging is a weapon.

Pads

Green pad = double points for 10s (everyone is told you took it). Cyan pads launch you to the high lanes.

Controls

W A S D move · Mouse aim · Click fire · Space jump · Touch & gamepad supported.
