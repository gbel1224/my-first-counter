# 🌴 Palm City

A GTA-style, top-down **open-world crime sandbox** that runs entirely in the browser — built as a single `index.html` with no dependencies. Play it on the live link below (best on mobile).

**▶ Play:** https://gbel1224.github.io/my-first-counter/

## The pitch
You arrive in a sunny pastel beach city with **$25**. Climb from odd jobs to owning the streets across a **12-chapter story** — steal cars, brawl, shoot your way through gangs, dodge a 5-star police heat system, and take down a recurring nemesis crime boss.

## Features
- **Open world** — a procedurally built city of roads, blocks, shops, parks, palm trees, a beach and ocean.
- **Drive or walk** — jack any car or bike (yank the driver out), momentum-based driving, roadkill, ramming.
- **Combat & 7 weapons** — fists, bat, pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle and RPG, with melee and gunplay.
- **Wanted system** — 0–5 ★ police heat; cops and cop-car chases spawn and escalate, then cool off if you break line of sight.
- **Living city** — dozens of pedestrians who wander, panic and flee; flowing traffic; ragdoll corpses; cash/armor pickups.
- **Explosions & rampage** — chained vehicle explosions with a **rampage multiplier** that pays out cash bonuses.
- **Economy** — earn cash from missions and mayhem, gain **XP**, level up to raise max health, buy a business.
- **Atmosphere** — day/night cycle, rain with wet reflective streets, headlights and night glow.
- **HUD** — Google-Maps-style circular **minimap**, health/armor bars, wanted stars, weapon + ammo, live clock.
- **Mobile-first** — virtual joystick + action/fire/melee/weapon/brake buttons; keyboard also supported on desktop.
- **Save persistence** — story progress auto-saves to `localStorage`; **Continue** picks up where you left off. **Free Roam** mode unlocks the full arsenal.

## Controls
| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move / steer | Left joystick | WASD / arrows |
| Enter / exit vehicle, pickups | ENTER | E |
| Fire weapon (hold) | FIRE | hold to shoot |
| Melee | 🥊 | Space |
| Switch weapon | WPN | Q |
| Brake (in car) | BRAKE | Space |

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it — no build step.
